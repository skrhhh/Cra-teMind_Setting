## Getting Started

1. Install from the App Store and open **CrateMind**.
2. Tap **+** to create your first box. The App assigns a readable code (e.g. `BK-001`) and an internal ID for the QR label.
3. Add items (one per line, or paste multiple lines). Optional: set expiration dates using formats like `Name|2026-12-31` or `Name|+90`.
4. Open the box detail page, generate the **QR label**, print or save it, and attach it to the physical box.
5. When you need something:
   - **Search** the item name from the box list, or  
   - Tap the center **Scan** tab and scan the label to open the box instantly.
6. Open **Settings (我的)** for Pro upgrade, restore purchase, iCloud sync, export/import, reminders, and legal links.

## Troubleshooting

- **Camera / scan does not work:** Allow camera access for CrateMind in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera**. Ensure you are scanning a label generated by this App (not a generic QR code).
- **“Not a CrateMind label” when scanning:** The QR must be created inside CrateMind. Labels from other apps cannot be opened.
- **Box not found after scan:** The box may have been deleted, or data has not synced to this device. Try searching by code (e.g. `BK-014`) or enable **iCloud sync (Pro)** on all devices with the same Apple ID.
- **iCloud sync not updating:** Pro is required. After toggling sync, **fully quit and reopen** the App as prompted. Confirm you are signed into iCloud on the device (**Settings → [your name] → iCloud**).
- **Hit the free box limit:** Upgrade to **Pro** from Settings or the paywall when creating a 7th box.
- **Pro not active after purchase:** Tap **Restore Purchase** in Settings. Ensure you use the same Apple ID as the original purchase.
- **Export / import issues:** Pro backup files use the `.cratemind` extension. Import merges by box ID; keep a safe copy of backups before importing on a device with existing data.
- **Expiration reminders not firing:** Enable **Expiration reminders** in Settings and allow notifications for CrateMind in iOS Settings.
- **Print layout looks wrong:** Try a different label size in Settings (Pro unlocks all styles). For AirPrint, use a compatible printer and check paper size (A4 batch print is Pro).
- **Data seems lost after reinstall:** Restore from **iCloud sync (Pro)** or a **`.cratemind` backup** you exported earlier. Device/iCloud backups may also help per Apple’s policies—we do not host your inventory on our servers.
- **Crashes or freezes:** Update to the latest App Store version; restart your device. If it persists, email us with device model, iOS version, and steps to reproduce.

- Uninstalling the App removes local App data from the device (subject to iOS backup behavior).
