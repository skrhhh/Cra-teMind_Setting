# CrateMind Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** June 1, 2026

The operator of the mobile application **CrateMind** (箱忆) (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect information related to your use of the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to information processed when you download and use **CrateMind** through the Apple App Store. It does not apply to third-party websites or services (for example, pages hosting this Policy), which are governed by their own policies.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide or That Is Generated Through Use

CrateMind is designed as a **local-first** storage organization tool. The following data is stored **on your device** (and optionally in **your iCloud account** if you enable sync) to support core features:

- Box records (name, location, readable code such as `BK-014`, color, notes, cover photos)
- Item lists inside each box (names, quantities, optional photos, expiration dates, taken-out status)
- QR code bindings (each code encodes a box identifier only—not the full item list)
- App preferences (label style/size, iCloud sync toggle, expiration reminder settings)
- Soft-deleted items in the recycle bin (until permanently removed)
- Pro purchase entitlement status (validated through Apple)

**We do not operate user accounts** and do **not** receive your box or item content on our servers. Content you enter stays on your device and, if enabled, in **your** iCloud storage under Apple’s policies.

### 2.2 Information We Do Not Collect

In the current version, we **do not**:

- Require registration, login, or a developer-hosted account
- Collect your real name, government ID, contacts, or precise location for our own databases
- Use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs
- Sell your personal information

### 2.3 Device Permissions

The App may request or use the following only for stated purposes:

| Permission / Capability | Purpose |
|-------------------------|---------|
| **Camera** | Scan CrateMind QR labels on storage boxes |
| **Photos / Camera (optional)** | Capture or choose cover photos for boxes and items |
| **Notifications (optional)** | Local expiration reminders you enable in Settings |
| **Network access** | App Store purchases, optional iCloud sync, and opening Privacy Policy / Terms / Support links |

You can review or revoke permissions in iOS **Settings** at any time. Core offline features (create boxes, edit lists, generate labels, scan codes) work without network access except where Apple services require connectivity (e.g. purchase validation, iCloud sync).

### 2.4 Apple Services

When you use Apple-provided services, Apple may process data under its own policies:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **App Store / StoreKit 2** | One-time **Pro** purchase and restore |
| **iCloud / CloudKit** (Pro, optional) | Sync boxes and items across your devices signed into the same Apple ID |
| **AirPrint** (Pro) | Print labels to compatible printers |

We do not receive your Apple ID password or payment card details. Purchase and billing are handled by Apple.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

1. Provide, maintain, and improve the App (box management, QR scan, search, labels, reminders)
2. Honor **Pro** features you purchased (unlimited boxes, sync, export/import, advanced printing, etc.)
3. Schedule local notifications for item expiration when you turn on reminders
4. Comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and protect our and users’ rights
5. Other purposes with your consent or as permitted by law

We **do not sell** your personal information to third parties.

## 5. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of CrateMind **does not display third-party ads** and **does not integrate analytics or crash-reporting SDKs** that send your inventory content to us or ad networks.

If we add optional analytics or advertising in a future version, we will update this Policy and, where required, obtain consent before such processing.

## 6. Storage and Security

- **Local data:** Stored in the App’s on-device database; removed when you uninstall the App (subject to iOS backup behavior).  
- **iCloud data:** Stored in your iCloud account when sync is enabled; governed by Apple’s security practices.  
- **Backups you create:** Pro users may export `.cratemind` backup files you control.  

We use reasonable safeguards in the App’s design (local-first, no developer cloud copy of your lists). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure—please keep your device and Apple ID secure.

---

## 7. Retention

- Box and item data: Until you delete it, empty the recycle bin, or uninstall the App (subject to iCloud and device backups).  
- Pro entitlement: As long as Apple’s purchase records show a valid non-consumable purchase on your Apple ID.  
- Longer retention where required by law.
