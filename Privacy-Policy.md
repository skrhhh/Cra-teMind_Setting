## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide or That Is Generated Through Use

Boxwise is designed as a **local-first** storage organization tool. The following data is stored **on your device** (and optionally in **your iCloud account** if you enable sync) to support core features:

- Box records (name, location, readable code such as `BK-014`, color, notes, cover photos)
- Item lists inside each box (names, quantities, optional photos, expiration dates, taken-out status)
- QR code bindings (each code encodes a box identifier only—not the full item list)
- App preferences (label style/size, iCloud sync toggle, expiration reminder settings, language)
- Soft-deleted items in the recycle bin (until permanently removed)
- Pro purchase or subscription entitlement status (validated through Apple)
- Ad-reward progress (e.g. temporary bonus box slots earned from rewarded video ads, stored locally)

**We do not operate user accounts** and do **not** receive your box or item content on our servers. Content you enter stays on your device and, if enabled, in **your** iCloud storage under Apple’s policies.

### 2.2 Automatically Collected Technical, Diagnostic, and Advertising Information

To display ads, measure usage, and improve stability, third-party SDKs may automatically collect information, including but not limited to:

- Device type, OS version, language/region settings
- App version and coarse usage signals (e.g. screen views, feature interactions, session events)
- Advertising identifiers (where you grant permission under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency; otherwise non-personalized identifiers may be used)
- IP address (often processed by ad/analytics providers for regional delivery, fraud prevention, and measurement)
- Crash logs, stack traces, and performance/diagnostic data (via Firebase Crashlytics and related transport)

We do **not** require your real name, government ID, payment card, precise home address, or contacts for the App. The App does **not** offer account registration or login with us.

### 2.3 Device Permissions and Consent Prompts

On **first launch**, the App may guide you through a short sequence (when applicable on your device and region): network availability, **App Tracking Transparency (ATT)**, and—in **GDPR/EEA and similar European regions**—a **Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** consent message for ads and related technologies.

The App may request or use the following only for stated purposes:

| Permission / Capability | Purpose |
|-------------------------|---------|
| **Camera** | Scan Boxwise QR labels on storage boxes |
| **Photos / Camera (optional)** | Capture or choose cover photos for boxes and items |
| **Notifications (optional)** | Local expiration reminders you enable in Settings |
| **Wireless / cellular data (network)** | Load ads, fetch UMP consent info, App Store purchases/subscriptions, optional iCloud sync, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics, and opening Privacy Policy / Terms / Support links |
| **Tracking (ATT, if prompted)** | Use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) for more relevant ads and measurement; you may decline |
| **UMP consent (European regions, when required)** | Record your choices about personalized ads and related technologies under applicable law |

You can review or change choices at any time:

- **Tracking:** iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**
- **Ad / cookie preferences (UMP):** **Settings** in the App → **Ad Privacy Choices** (shown when required)
- **Camera, Photos, Notifications:** iOS **Settings → Privacy & Security**

Core offline features (create boxes, edit lists, generate labels, scan codes, search) work without network access except where Apple or third-party services require connectivity (e.g. purchase validation, iCloud sync, ad loading).

### 2.4 Apple Services

When you use Apple-provided services, Apple may process data under its own policies:

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **App Store / StoreKit 2** | Pro subscriptions, lifetime purchase, restore purchases |
| **iCloud / CloudKit** (Pro, optional) | Sync boxes and items across your devices signed into the same Apple ID |
| **AirPrint** (Pro) | Print labels to compatible printers |

We do not receive your Apple ID password or payment card details. Purchase and billing are handled by Apple.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

1. Provide, maintain, and improve the App (box management, QR scan, search, labels, reminders, sync, export/import)
2. Honor **Pro** features you purchased or subscribed to (unlimited boxes, ad removal, sync, export/import, advanced printing, etc.)
3. Display **app open** and **rewarded video** ads to free-tier users and grant ad-based benefits (e.g. temporary extra box slots or access to certain Pro-style actions when offered)
4. Measure aggregate usage and diagnose crashes to improve reliability (Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics)
5. Schedule local notifications for item expiration when you turn on reminders
6. Comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and protect our and users’ rights
7. Other purposes with your consent or as permitted by law

We **do not sell** your personal information to third parties.

## 5. Advertising, Analytics, and Your Choices

### 5.1 Advertising (Free Tier)

The free version is supported by ads, which may include:

- **App open ads** on cold or hot launch (Pro subscribers generally do not see ads)
- **Rewarded video ads** that may grant temporary benefits (e.g. an extra box slot, or access to batch print / AirPrint flows when offered in the App)

**ATT:** If you allow tracking, AdMob and partners may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) for personalization and measurement. If you decline, they may rely on non-personalized or contextual signals permitted by Apple and their policies.

**UMP (where shown):** In European regions where we enable UMP, you will see a Google-managed consent flow. Your choices determine whether the App may request **personalized** ads. You can update choices later via **Ad Privacy Choices** in the App’s Settings when that option is available.

**Effect on the App:** Core offline organization features remain available if you decline ATT or do not grant ad consent. Some ads may not load, or may be less relevant or non-personalized, until applicable consent is given or as allowed by law.

### 5.2 Analytics and Crash Reporting

Firebase Analytics helps us understand how features are used in aggregate. Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic data to fix bugs. This data is not used to read your box or item contents.

You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings and ad consent via UMP as described above. Uninstalling the App stops further local data collection by the App.

## 7. Storage and Security

- **Local data:** Stored in the App’s on-device database; removed when you uninstall the App (subject to iOS backup behavior).
- **iCloud data:** Stored in your iCloud account when sync is enabled; governed by Apple’s security practices.
- **Backups you create:** Pro users may export `.cratemind` backup files you control.
- **Ad/analytics data:** Processed per Google’s policies on their infrastructure.

We use reasonable safeguards in the App’s design (local-first inventory, no developer cloud copy of your lists). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure—please keep your device and Apple ID secure.

## 8. Retention

- Box and item data: Until you delete it, empty the recycle bin, or uninstall the App (subject to iCloud and device backups).
- Pro entitlement: As long as Apple’s records show a valid subscription or purchase on your Apple ID.

- Longer retention where required by law.
